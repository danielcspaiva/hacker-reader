A complete redesign. Clean white cards in Light Mode, warm charcoal in Dark Mode, and orange accents that match the app icon.

• New Home Screen widgets that put the #1 story front and center, with more top stories alongside. They fill every size cleanly and stay up to date even with the app closed
• A new Lock Screen widget with the top three stories
• Numbered story cards with a square thumbnail and the site's icon
• Story pages with a serif headline and a large link preview. The title moves into the navigation bar as you scroll. Tap the points to upvote
• Tap any comment to collapse or expand its replies
• Comment threads with quiet orange depth rails, much faster on stories with 1,000+ comments
• A Liquid Glass compose button, and a comment box that sits right on the keyboard
• Switch between Top, New, Ask, Show and Jobs from the menu in the top-right corner
• Tap a comment in someone's submissions to jump to it in the thread
• A launch screen that matches Light and Dark Mode
• Blocking a comment author now asks first

Fixes
• Upvoting a story you had already voted on showed "Something Went Wrong"
• Clearer messages when Hacker News asks you to slow down, verify you're human or sign in again
• A storage error could erase saved bookmarks, votes or blocked users
• Posting a comment could wrongly report a failure
• The comment box stayed locked for several seconds after posting
• Voting or commenting on some threads could sign you out
• Network errors showed "No stories found" instead of a Try again button
• VoiceOver now reads each story card's title, site and counts
