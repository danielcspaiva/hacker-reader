DEMO ACCOUNT
Credentials are entered in App Store Connect and are not stored in this repository.

Only voting, commenting, flagging and submitting need an account. Bookmarks, hidden stories, blocked users, mutes and reading history are stored on the device.

TESTING WITHOUT LOGIN
1. Feeds
- Opens on Stories (Top). Tabs: Stories, Bookmarks, Profile, Settings, Search.
- Switch feeds with the header menu: Top, Best, New, Ask, Show, Jobs, plus Past Front Pages.
- Past Front Pages opens on yesterday. Previous and next step through days. Next is disabled on today.
- Pull down to refresh; scroll to load more.
- Open a story, go back: its title is dimmed. Long-press the card for Mark as Read / Mark as Unread.

2. Story page
- Tap a card to open a story. On a link post, tap the link preview to read the article in an in-app browser.
- Header: Bookmark and More (Share, Open in Browser on link posts, Hide, Mute site, Flag, Block User).
- Touch and hold a story card: Upvote, Bookmark, Share, Mark as Read, More > Hide / Mute site / Flag / Block User.
- Comment tools: jump to the previous or next top-level comment, and Collapse all.
- Settings > Text Size changes the thread. Settings > Muted Words & Sites adds a keyword or a site.

3. Comments
- Tap the header of a comment with replies to collapse the thread. Tap again to expand.
- A comment's More button: Block User. Signed in, also Reply (Delete Comment on your own).

4. Search: type a query or tap a suggestion. The header menu sets sort (Relevance / Newest), scope (Stories / Comments), date range and minimum points. author:name limits results to that user.
5. Bookmarks: saved stories. More > Clear All Bookmarks empties the list. Muted stories still appear here.
6. User profiles: tap a username for karma, member since, bio and Submissions.
7. Settings: Appearance; Text Size; Muted Words & Sites; Blocked Users; Hidden Posts; Clear Reading History; Clear Cache; Clear Bookmarks.

8. iPad
- On an iPad (full screen, or a wide Split View) the list stays on the left and the selected story opens on the right. Tapping a card does not push a new screen.
- On iPhone, tapping a card still pushes the story.

TESTING WITH LOGIN
9. Sign in
- Profile tab > Sign in.
- First time only, the Hacker News Guidelines sheet appears: tap Accept & Continue.
- Enter the demo credentials from App Store Connect and tap Sign in.
- Profile then shows the demo account and its karma.

For 10-12 use a recent Top or New story. HN blocks votes on job posts, and comments on job posts or threads over two weeks old.

10. Upvote (stories only)
- Tap the points pill on a story page, or long-press a card > Upvote. The pill turns orange. Tap again, or Unvote, to remove it.
- Signed out, this shows Sign In Required.

11. Comment
- Tap the compose button, type, and send. Reply from a comment's More menu. Delete your own comment from that same menu.

12. Submit
- Profile > Submit, or the feed menu. A signed-out attempt asks you to sign in.
- Flag (a story with few comments): More > Flag. The demo account has karma 1, so HN returns Insufficient Karma. Hide and Block User work regardless.

13. Sign out: Profile > Sign out > confirm. Browsing still works.

TESTING iOS WIDGETS
Open the app once first. Before that, widgets may show a prompt to open Hacker Reader.
14. Home Screen: add the HN Top Stories widget.
- Small: the top story. Medium: two stories. Large: seven. Tap a row to open that story.
15. Lock Screen: the widget shows the top three headlines.
Widgets refresh on their own and when the app opens.

COMMON ISSUES & NOTES
- Accounts are created on news.ycombinator.com. The app has no sign-up and keeps only the HN username and session cookie (Keychain) until sign out.
- All content is hosted by Hacker News (Y Combinator), not this app.
- No paid features, subscriptions or in-app purchases.
- Submit a story from Profile. This build has no iOS share extension.
